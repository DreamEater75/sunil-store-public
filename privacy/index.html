Sunil Store — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

This policy explains what personal information Sunil Store collects, why, who else sees it, where it is kept and for how long, and what you can ask us to do with it. It covers the Sunil Store app (Android package com.sunilstore.customer) and the store's website, where this policy and the app's download page live.

Who we are. The app and the store are run by Sunil Store, Old Market, Hirakud, Odisha, 768016. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (the "DPDP Act") we are the Data Fiduciary: we decide why and how your information is used, and we are responsible for it.

In short. We collect what we need to bring your order to your door. We don't sell your information, we don't show ads, and the app has no advertising or analytics trackers. You can delete your account yourself, from inside the app.

1. What we collect

When you sign in with Google: your name, your email address, and a number that identifies your Google account to our app. We never receive your Google password, contacts, or anything else in your Google account.

Your mobile number, which the app asks for with your first delivery address (or at checkout) so the store and the person delivering can call you. We don't verify it, and we don't use it for marketing calls or messages.

Your delivery addresses: the name and mobile number of the person receiving the order, flat or house details, street, landmark, area, city and pincode. If you tap Use my current location while adding an address, the app reads your phone's location once, and, after you confirm it is the delivery address, saves that point with the address so we can find the door. The app never reads your location at any other time, and never in the background.

Your orders: what you ordered, prices, taxes, delivery and packaging charges, any offer or coupon you used, any note you add, the order's status history, and the invoice. How you paid: cash, or UPI at your door. We record the amount, how it was paid and when; your UPI or bank details stay in your own UPI app and never reach us.

Your store wallet: credits such as refunds and referral rewards, and what you spend from it.

Your confirmation that an order arrived. When you press I've received my order, we record the time, which of your signed-in sessions pressed it, and the app version. This settles any later question about whether an order arrived, for you and for the store.

Your reviews, if you rate something from a delivered order: the stars, any title and words you write, and up to three photos.

Returns, if you report a problem with a delivered order: which items, the reason, any note, and up to three photos.

Messages to the store written in the app under Help & support (including Not received yet?): the topic, your message, the order it's about, and the store's reply. If you message the store on WhatsApp or call it, that conversation happens outside our app and we don't record it.

Saved items and alerts: products you save for later, and products you ask to hear about when they're back in stock.

Referrals: your referral code and, if you joined with a friend's code, whose code it was and when. We never see who you share your code with.

A record of the notifications we send you: what was sent and when.

From your phone and your use of the app:

  • a random identifier the app creates when first opened, so a cart works before you sign in. Before you sign in it isn't linked to who you are; when you sign in, that cart joins your account;
  • your signed-in sessions: when each started and was last used, the IP address, and the kind of device and app as your phone reports them, because an account can be signed in on one phone at a time;
  • what you search for in the app and how many results it found, so we can stock what people look for;
  • if you allow notifications, a notification token for your phone, so we can tell you about your orders and, sometimes, offers;
  • technical logs our server keeps for each request, such as the time, the address requested, your IP address, the app version and the app's random identifier, used for security and to fix problems.

Permissions the app asks for, each only when you use the feature: location (once, when you tap Use my current location), photos (only the ones you pick for a review or a return, through your phone's own picker), and notifications. You can refuse any of them and still order.

What we don't collect: your contacts, other photos or files, messages, call logs, your location in the background, or any card, bank or UPI details.

2. Why we use it

  • To provide the service: take your order, pack it, deliver it, collect payment, and keep you updated.
  • To keep accounts: record payments, wallet credits and refunds, and issue invoices.
  • To keep your account secure: one signed-in phone per customer, limits on repeated requests, and detecting misuse.
  • To settle questions about delivery, using the receipt record described above.
  • To meet legal obligations: tax and accounting records, and answering authorities where the law requires.
  • To improve the store: search terms tell us what to stock.
  • To show reviews you choose to write on the product's page.
  • To send offers, only as notifications and only if you allow them. We may choose who gets an offer by when they last ordered (for example, customers who haven't ordered in a month). Offers have their own notification category on your phone, which you can switch off while still getting order updates.

We process your information with your consent, which you give when you sign in and use the app, and for the purposes the DPDP Act allows without separate consent, such as using information you gave us voluntarily for the service you asked for, and keeping records the law requires. We don't make automated decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

3. Who else sees it

The store. The owner and staff see orders, addresses and contact details, because they pack and send your order. What they can see depends on their role, and every change they make in the store's admin panel is recorded.

The person delivering your order receives a WhatsApp message from the store with the order number, the receiver's name and mobile number, the delivery address, a map link, and the amount to collect. Not what you ordered. They don't use our app.

You see the delivery person's name and mobile number while your order is on the way, so you can call them.

Referrals. If you join with a friend's code, you see their first name. They see only how many friends have joined with their code and how many have had an order delivered; never your name, number, or what you ordered.

Reviews are public. Once published, anyone using the app can see a review's stars, title, words and photos, its date, and your first name with the first letter of your last name (or "Verified buyer"). Never your number, email or address. Stars alone are published at once; a review with words or photos is checked by the store first.

Authorities. We disclose information to police, courts, tax or other authorities when Indian law requires it.

We never sell your information and never share it for advertising.

5. Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app: Account → Delete account.

  1. You are signed out at once, and any of your sessions still open stops working.
  2. For 14 days nothing is erased, but your reviews are hidden from other shoppers straight away. If you change your mind, sign in again within those 14 days and your account is restored as it was.
  3. After 14 days we permanently erase your name, email, mobile number, Google account identifier, saved addresses, cart, saved items and alerts, notification tokens and the record of notifications sent, sessions, messages to the store, return notes and photos, and your reviews with their photos. We keep your orders only as the records described in section 4, with your name, number, email and street address removed; only the city and pincode remain, which an invoice needs.
  4. Copies in our encrypted nightly backups are deleted within the following 14 days.

Two things can delay deletion:

  • An order in progress: erasure waits until it is delivered or cancelled.
  • Money in your store wallet: the app asks you to use it on an order or ask us for a refund first, so you don't lose money you are owed.

You can sign up again later with the same Google account, but you will start with a new, empty account.

6. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can:

  • ask what personal information we hold about you, how we use it, and who we have shared it with;
  • correct or complete information that is wrong or incomplete. You can edit your delivery addresses, and each one's contact number, yourself in the app; to change the mobile number on your account, contact our Grievance Officer;
  • have your information erased (section 5), except what the law requires us to keep;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, as easily as you gave it: delete your account, or switch off notifications. This stops the parts of the service that need it, and doesn't affect what was lawfully done before;
  • nominate someone to use these rights for you if you die or can no longer use them yourself;
  • have a complaint answered by our Grievance Officer (section 10), and, if you're not satisfied, complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

To use any of these rights, contact our Grievance Officer. We may need to confirm it's really you, usually by asking you to sign in to the app.

7. Children

The Sunil Store app is for adults: you must be 18 or older to create an account. If we learn that we hold the personal information of someone under 18 without a parent's or guardian's verifiable consent, we will delete it.

8. Keeping it safe

  • Everything between the app and our server travels encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Our database can be reached only by our own server; its host's public data interface is switched off for every table.
  • Return photos are stored privately. Staff open them through links that stop working after ten minutes. Product images and review photos are stored where anyone with their web address can see them, because they're meant to be seen; a review photo's address is given to no one but you and the store until the review is published.
  • Staff access depends on their role, and staff changes are recorded in an audit log.
  • No passwords or secret keys are built into the app.
  • Our nightly backups are encrypted with a passphrase the store controls.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal information, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board of India, as the law requires.

Installing the app safely. We distribute the app ourselves, not through the Google Play Store. Install it only from our download page, which lists a checksum for each file so it can be checked. Never install a copy someone forwards to you.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a way that matters, such as collecting something new or sharing with someone new, we will tell you in the app before the change takes effect. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
