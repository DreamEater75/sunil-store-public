Sunil Store — Delivery Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Where we deliver. Only within our delivery area around the store. The app checks your address when you save it and at checkout, and won't take an order we can't deliver.

When. We take orders during store hours, 7:30 am to 11 pm. Choose the earliest delivery, or pick a delivery slot where the app offers one.

What it costs. The delivery charge and the packaging charge depend on your order's total weight, in bands the store sets; delivery may be free above an order value. The store also sets the heaviest order it accepts: if your cart is heavier, the cart shows the limit and won't go to checkout until it's lighter. All of this, and any minimum order value, is shown before you place the order, and it stays as agreed even if items come off your order at packing.

When your order is on its way. We notify you, and your order screen shows the estimated arrival time and the name and mobile number of the person bringing it, so you can call them. If they're running late, we update the time and tell you. If the time passes and nothing has arrived, press Not received yet? on the order and we'll look into it straight away.

Receiving your order. Please pay the exact amount shown, in cash or to the store's UPI QR. Then press I've received my order in the app (Terms of Use).

If we can't deliver. If no one is available, the address can't be found, or the order is refused, we record the reason and may try again. If the order finally comes back to the store, anything you paid from your wallet goes back to it, as set out in the Refund and Cancellation Policy.
