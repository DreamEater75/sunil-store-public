Your account. You sign in with your Google account, and your account can be signed in on one phone at a time. What you receive. Stock is checked when you order, and an item that runs out on the shelf before we pick it is taken off your bill. You choose at checkout what happens instead:

  • Allow a similar item. We may send a comparable product, charged at the price of the item you ordered, never more.

  • Refund the item. We leave it out.

Items sold by weight. Fruit, vegetables and other loose items are weighed when packed, and the price follows the actual weight. A small difference, within a limit the store sets, is applied automatically. For anything larger, we call you first, and if you decline, the item is left out and not charged. If part of your order was paid from your wallet, any increase is collected at the door, and your order screen shows the amount.

Paying. You pay on delivery: in cash, or by UPI to the store's own QR code, which the delivery person shows you. Please pay only to the store's QR, never to a personal number. Your order screen shows the exact amount. Money in your Sunil Store wallet can be used on any order. It isn't cash, can't be transferred to anyone else, and doesn't expire.

Confirming you received your order. When your order arrives, press I've received my order in the app, only once the items are in your hands. We record when you pressed it. If something is wrong, press Received, but there's a problem instead and tell us what (Refund and Cancellation Policy).
