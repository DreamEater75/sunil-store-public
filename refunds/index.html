Sunil Store — Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Cancelling your order. You can cancel from the order's screen in the app until we start picking it. Once picking begins, the Cancel option disappears. Call us on +91 70085 31127 and we can still cancel it, up until it leaves the store. After that it can't be cancelled.

When we cancel. We may cancel an order we can't fulfil, for example if the items are out of stock or your address is outside our delivery area. We'll tell you why.

How refunds work. Refunds go to your Sunil Store wallet, instantly and automatically, the moment the reason for them happens:

What happened What comes back
You or we cancelled an order you had paid for, in part, from your wallet Everything taken from the wallet
An item was out of stock, or we could only pack part of it The price of what we couldn't send, when your order is packed
You declined a weight-based price increase That item's full price
We couldn't deliver, and the order came back to the store The full amount you paid

Nothing is paid until your order arrives, so for the rest there's nothing to refund: you simply pay for what is actually delivered, and your order screen shows the correct amount.

A problem with a delivered order. If something is missing, damaged, past its date, the wrong item, or of poor quality, bring it to the store within 24 hours of delivery: Sunil Store, Old Market, Hirakud, open 7:30 am to 11 pm. Refunds for a delivered order are given only at the store, once we have seen the item. For a missing item there's nothing to bring; just come with your order number. You can tell us first, so we're ready: open the order in the app and tap "Return or report a problem" (choose the items, say what's wrong, add a photo if you can), call us on +91 70085 31127, or write to santoshgoyal711@gmail.com. We check the items at the counter and refund what we accept, in cash or to your wallet, as you prefer. A coupon discount on the order is shared across its items, so an item's refund is its price less its share of the discount. The delivery fee isn't refunded when only some items come back. If we can't accept a request, the app tells you why. Perishable items can't be returned once accepted, but a genuine quality problem is always refunded.

Your wallet balance can be used on any future order and never expires. If you'd rather have it back, ask us on +91 70085 31127 and we'll send it to your UPI or pay it at the counter. See the Terms of Use.
