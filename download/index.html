How to install

  1. Tap Download APK below.
  2. When the download finishes, open the file.
  3. If your phone asks, allow Install unknown apps for this browser. You only do this once.
  4. If Play Protect shows a warning, tap Install anyway. It appears because the app is not from the Play Store.
  5. Open the app and sign in with your Google account.

Updating: open this page again and download the new version. It installs over the old one, and you stay signed in.
